MY PRODUCT / APPLIED AI

AI-Powered Interview Preparation

Personalized practice.
Actionable feedback.

An AI-powered interview practice product that turns a candidate’s CV and target job description into tailored questions, guided practice, and a clear path to stronger answers.

CV-based personalizationVoice interactionAI coaching

The problem

Generic question banks miss the connection between a candidate’s experience and the role they want. Candidates need relevant practice and specific guidance on how to improve.

The product experience

  1. Personalize. Build a profile from a CV and add a job description to shape the interview.
  2. Practice. Answer tailored questions with Maya, the AI interviewer, using voice or text.
  3. Improve. Review coaching insights, stronger example answers, and a report covering content relevance, communication clarity, and confidence.

Product thinking in action

A connected workflow from candidate context to interview practice and feedback, with explicit consent steps before CV processing and AI evaluation.
